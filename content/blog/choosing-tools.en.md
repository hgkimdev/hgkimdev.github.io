The things I check when looking at a new tool are mostly fixed. Fixing even the order helps — it cuts down on just counting up reasons to like it first.

1. Can I write the problem I'm facing right now in a single sentence?
2. Why can't my current tools solve that problem?
3. What would it take to undo this after adopting it?

The third one gets skipped most often. A tool adopted without knowing the cost of reversing it ends up staying in use even after problems show up later.
