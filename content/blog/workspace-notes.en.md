If I rely on memory for what got better each time I change my setup, I end up buying the same thing again later. It's better to write down what changed and the result as it happens.

## What changed

- Raising the monitor to eye level reduced the strain on my neck
- Switching keyboards had nothing to do with my wrists. Height was the issue, not the layout

## What stayed the same

The actual working hours stayed about the same regardless of the tools. The environment only makes sitting for a long time less taxing — it doesn't increase the total amount of time I spend focused.
