I've changed how I memorize words several times but never compared the results. This time I'm writing down what changed and what it did.

## What changed

- Pulling new words straight from the sentence I just read, instead of batching them into a wordbook, made them stick longer
- I stopped setting a fixed number of words per day — if review is backed up, I don't add new ones

## What stayed the same

Pronunciation didn't improve no matter how many words I memorized, unless I practiced it separately. Knowing a word and being able to pronounce it correctly are two different problems.
